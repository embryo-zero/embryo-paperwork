# Embryo Paperwork

Embryo Paperwork is Embryo-Zero's fork of [paperless-ngx](https://github.com/paperless-ngx/paperless-ngx) (28bb638, commit 28bb638). It does scans and files documents.

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (LICENSE) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
